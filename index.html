10 million learners in South Africa walk to school every day, and many make their journey in worn-out shoes or with no shoes at all.

Without proper footwear, children face a higher risk of injury and infection, and sometimes miss school as a result.

Our mission is to change the footprint of disadvantaged children across South Africa.

WHAT WE DO

Barefoot No More™ and Kids Etc NPC work together.

  • Partner with companies, retailers and individual donors to fund school shoe packages
  • Help our partners find the schools where children need shoes most
  • Send our Shoe Crews to each school with our partners, making sure every child receives a pair that fits them perfectly
  • Offer extras such as branded book bags, stationery sets and school uniforms

OUR SHOES

Many of the children we reach have never owned a new pair of shoes, let alone had the means to look after them. So Barefoot No More™ designed a school shoe that needs almost no care. Our unique, patent-pending shoe is made in South Africa from a tough, fully recyclable material, built to handle harsh rural conditions day after day.

  • Waterproof
  • Shock-absorbent
  • No polish needed
  • Won't cause blisters
  • Stretches to accomodate a growing foot
  • Fully recyclable
