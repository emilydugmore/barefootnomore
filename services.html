OUR SERVICES

School shoe packages, uniforms and fully organised giveaways, so every child can take vital steps towards a better education.

SCHOOL SHOE PACKAGES

Choose from three package options, from a pair of shoes on its own up to a full back-to-school set.

Option 1

School Shoes

A pair of school shoes.

Option 2

Shoes + Book Bag

A pair of school shoes and a branded drawstring book bag.

Option 3 · Most popular

Shoes + Book Bag + Stationery

A pair of school shoes, a branded drawstring book bag and a branded stationery set.

BAG & STATIONERY COLOURS

Drawstring book bags and stationery sets come in a range of colours and can be branded with your logo. Stationery sets include a pencil bag with a pen, pencil, ruler, eraser and sharpener.

ORGANISED SCHOOL SHOE GIVEAWAYS

Our Kids Etc NPC Shoe Crews visit the school with the donor to hand out the packages, making sure every child receives a pair of shoes that fits them perfectly. We also offer promotional items for your staff (branded clothing, caps, banners, etc.) and extra add-ons for the children on the day (lunchboxes, bottles, etc.).
